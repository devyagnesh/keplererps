11.2 Database conventions
Convention Rule
T able names Plural, lower snake case, prefixed by module where helpful: sales_orders, 
sales_order_items, stock_ledger_entries.
Primary key id, unsigned big integer, auto-increment.
Foreign keys {singular_table}_id with a real foreign key constraint, ON DELETE RESTRICT for
masters and ON DELETE CASCADE for lines belonging to a header.
Timestamps created_at, updated_at, deleted_at on every table, plus created_by and 
updated_by on transaction tables.
Money and quantity DECIMAL(18,2) for money, DECIMAL(18,4) for quantity and rate, DECIMAL(9,4) for
percentages. Never FLOAT or DOUBLE.
Status columns Short string values backed by a PHP enum, not integers — a database dump should be
readable without a lookup table.
JSON columns Used only for custom field values, industry attributes and log properties. Anything that
must be filtered or joined gets a real column.
