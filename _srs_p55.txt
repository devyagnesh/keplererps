US-M12-02 — As a Dispatch Officer, I want e-way bill fields validated before the vehicle leaves, so that the
truck is not stopped at a checkpoint.
Acceptance: when the invoice value exceeds the threshold, transport mode, vehicle number, distance and
transporter ID become mandatory; vehicle number format is validated; the e-way bill JSON is downloadable
and, where the GSP integration is enabled, submitted directly.
US-M12-03 — As a Customer, I want a WhatsApp message when my material is dispatched, so that I can
arrange to receive it.
Acceptance: alert N13 sends invoice number, vehicle number, transporter, LR number, e-way bill number and
expected delivery date, with the invoice PDF attached.
US-M12-04 — As an Accountant, I want the challan and the invoice to always agree, so that GST returns
reconcile.
Acceptance: an invoice generated from a challan copies quantities exactly and locks them; a difference is
only possible through a documented amendment before the invoice is confirmed.
