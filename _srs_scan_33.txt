Business rules
# Rule
BR-01 A master record that is referenced by any transaction can never be deleted — only made inactive. The delete
button shows the count of blocking references.
BR-02 GSTIN uniqueness is checked across the whole party table, ignoring soft-deleted rows, and the error message
names the conflicting party.
BR-03 Changing a party's state after transactions exist raises a warning, because it changes IGST versus CGST/SGST
behaviour on future documents. Past documents keep their original tax treatment.
BR-04 Warehouses form a tree; a parent cannot be deleted while children exist, and stock is only ever held at leaf
level.
BR-05 The company record is a singleton — exactly one row, created by the installer, never deletable.
MODULE M02
User, Role and Permission Management
Purpose
Creates the people who use the system and controls exactly what each of them may do and see. The
detailed model is described in Section 5; this module is the screens that operate it.
How it works
An admin creates a user, links them optionally to an employee record, assigns one or more roles, and sets
a data scope. The permission matrix screen shows a grid of modules down the side and actions across the
top, with a tick box in every cell and "select all" controls for a row or a column. Changes take effect on the
user's next request — the permission cache is cleared immediately for that user.
