7. Notification Engine — WhatsApp and Firebase
7.1 Why this matters
In a real factory, people do not sit in front of the ERP . The store keeper is at the gate, the supervisor is on
the shop floor, the owner is travelling. An ERP that requires people to log in to discover a problem will not
change how the business runs. So the ERP must push information out — to WhatsApp for people outside
the system (customers, suppliers, transporters) and to push notifications for people inside it (staff with the
app or browser open).
