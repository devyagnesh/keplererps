Requirements common to every report
# Requirement Detail
R1 Consistent filters Date range, branch, warehouse, item category, party group and status filters
behave identically in every report. Filter selections are remembered per user.
R2 Drill-down everywhere Any total can be clicked to reveal the transactions behind it, and any
transaction opens its source document.
R3 Export Excel, CSV and PDF from every report. Exports respect field permissions — a
user who cannot see rates does not get a rate column in the file.
R4 Scheduling Any report can be scheduled daily, weekly or monthly and delivered by email
or WhatsApp to a list of recipients.
R5 Performance Reports covering up to one year of data must render within 5 seconds. Heavier
reports run as queued jobs and notify the user when ready.
R6 Scope-aware Every report obeys the user's data scope. A branch user's "total sales" is their
branch's total, and the report header states the scope applied.
