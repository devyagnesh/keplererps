MODULE M08
Inventory and Warehouse
Purpose
Holds the truth about what material exists, where it is, in which batch, and what it is worth. Every other
module either reads from it or writes to it, and nothing writes to it except through the stock ledger.
How it works
Stock is never stored as a number that someone can type over. Instead, every movement writes a row into
the  stock  ledger —  item,  warehouse,  batch,  quantity  in  or  out,  rate,  value,  source  document  and
timestamp. The current balance is the sum of the ledger, kept in a cached balance table that is updated
inside the same database transaction. This design means stock can always be explained: clicking a balance
shows every movement that produced it.
Four quantities are tracked separately and must never be confused:
Quantity Meaning
Physical What is actually lying in the warehouse right now.
Committed Physically present but already promised to a confirmed sales order or reserved for a work order.
On order Not yet arrived, but covered by an approved purchase order or a running work order.
Available (free) Physical − committed. This is the number a sales executive must see before promising delivery.
