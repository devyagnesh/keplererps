10.17.4 Scanning — one code, several actions
Figure 10.6 — From scan to action. The same code drives receiving, moving, picking, dispatching and enquiry.
Stock in
Three situations produce a stock-in scan. From production: after the batch passes QC (M10), the operator
packs it, the system creates the packing units, the labels print, and scanning them at the finished-goods
store posts the receipt into stock. From purchase: incoming material is labelled at the goods-in point after
GRN, so raw material drums are traceable through production the same way. From a customer return:
scanning a returned box brings it back into a quarantine location pending re-inspection, never straight into
saleable stock.
Stock out and dispatch
The dispatch officer opens the delivery challan on a phone or tablet and scans each package as it goes
onto the vehicle. The screen shows a live count of loaded against required, per item. The value of this is not
the speed — it is that the wrong box gets caught at the tailgate instead of at the customer's gate. These
are the checks the system runs on every loading scan:
