7.4.2 Template management
WhatsApp only allows business-initiated messages using templates approved by Meta. The ERP therefore
keeps a local whatsapp_templates table that mirrors the approved templates and maps each ERP alert to
one of them.
Field Rule
template_name Exactly as approved in Meta, lower snake case. Required, unique.
language_code e.g. en, en_US, gu, hi. Required.
category UTILITY / MARKETING / AUTHENTICATION. Transactional ERP alerts must be UTILITY .
body_variables Ordered list mapping {{1}}, {{2}} … to ERP placeholders such as {customer_name}, 
{document_no}, {amount}.
has_document_headerBoolean. When true the alert attaches the generated PDF as the header document.
status APPROVED / PENDING / REJECTED, refreshed by a "Sync templates" button that reads the
Meta API.
Common mistake to avoid. Do not build free-text WhatsApp messages for outbound alerts. They will only be
delivered inside a 24-hour customer service window and will silently fail otherwise. Always send an approved
template, and use free text only when replying inside an open conversation.
