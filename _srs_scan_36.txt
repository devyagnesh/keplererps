US-M03-05 — As a Production Planner, I want to define alternative raw materials for an item, so that
production is not stopped when the preferred grade is unavailable.
Acceptance: an item may list substitute items with a conversion ratio; the material issue screen offers
substitutes with a visible warning and records which substitute was used.
