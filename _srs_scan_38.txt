US-M04-02 — As a Production Planner, I want to create a new version of a BOM when the design changes,
so that old work orders still show what was actually used.
Acceptance: versions are numbered automatically; existing open work orders keep their original version; new
work orders pick the active version by date.
US-M04-03 — As a Costing user, I want the system to calculate the standard cost of a finished item from its
BOM, so that I know whether the selling price is profitable.
Acceptance: cost = sum of component costs (at the chosen valuation) + operation costs (machine rate ×
time + labour rate × time) + configurable overhead percentage; a "rolled-up cost" view explodes sub-
assemblies.
US-M04-04 — As a Production Planner, I want to mark an operation as outsourced, so that the system
creates a job-work challan instead of a shop-floor task.
Acceptance: outsourced operations require a vendor and a rate; releasing the work order generates a job-
work issue with material sent out, and receipt against it reconciles quantity and scrap.
US-M04-05 — As a Production Planner, I want to record by-products and scrap that come out of the
process, so that regrind and off-cuts return to stock with a value.
Acceptance: a BOM may list output items besides the main product, each with an expected quantity and a
cost-allocation percentage; production entry receives them into stock automatically.
