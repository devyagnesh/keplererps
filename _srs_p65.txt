Opening and splitting
Real dispatches are not always whole boxes. T wo actions handle this, and both are deliberate rather than
silent. Open breaks the seal: the unit's status becomes Opened, its contents return to loose stock in the
same location, and the code is retired. Split takes part of the contents into a new unit with a new code,
reducing the original — used when half a drum goes to one customer. Neither action changes total stock
quantity. Both are logged with the user, the reason and the before-and-after contents, because a box that
quietly loses ten kilograms is how inventory drifts.
