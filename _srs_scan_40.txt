User stories
US-M05-01 — As a Sales Executive, I want a reminder on my phone for today's follow-ups, so that I do not
lose an enquiry by forgetting to call.
Acceptance: a push notification each morning listing today's follow-ups with a deep link; overdue follow-ups
are highlighted in red on the lead list.
US-M05-02 — As a Sales Manager, I want duplicate leads detected automatically, so that two executives do
not call the same customer.
Acceptance: on save, the system checks mobile number, email and company name against existing leads and
customers, and shows possible matches with an option to merge or continue.
US-M05-03 — As a Sales Manager, I want to import leads from an IndiaMART export, so that enquiries reach
the team the same day.
Acceptance: a mapping screen matches file columns to system fields, remembers the mapping for next time,
validates rows, and reports imported and rejected counts.
US-M05-04 — As an Owner, I want a funnel report showing how many leads are in each stage and the
conversion percentage per executive, so that I can see who is performing.
Acceptance: a funnel chart with drill-down, filterable by date range, source, executive and product category.
