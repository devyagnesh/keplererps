MODULE M12
Dispatch, Packing and Logistics
Purpose
Gets the finished goods out of the gate correctly and legally: packed and labelled, with a delivery challan,
an e-way bill where required, and the customer told that their material has left.
How it works
Dispatch begins with a  packing list built against a sales order: which items, which batches or serials,
packed into how many cartons, bundles, bags or pallets, with gross and net weight. Packing generates
labels with QR codes so each package can be identified; the packing unit, its code, its label and every scan
against it are specified in full in M17, and the package count on the challan is derived from scanned units
rather  than  typed.  The  delivery  challan is  then  created  from  the  packing  list,  reducing  stock  and
recording the vehicle, driver, transporter and LR number. Where the invoice value and distance require it,
e-way bill details are captured and the JSON payload for the government portal is prepared. Once the
vehicle leaves, alert N13 goes to the customer on WhatsApp with the vehicle number and expected arrival,
and proof of delivery can be uploaded when the signed challan returns.
User stories
US-M12-01 — As a Dispatch Officer, I want to build a packing list by scanning batch labels, so that the
packing list matches what is physically in the truck.
Acceptance: scanning a batch label adds that batch to the current package; the system warns if the batch is
not from the linked sales order's reserved stock; package totals update live.
