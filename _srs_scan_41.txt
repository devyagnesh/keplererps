MODULE M06
Sales — Quotation to Invoice
Purpose
Runs the complete order-to-cash cycle: quoting a price, taking a confirmed order, reserving or producing
the goods, delivering them and raising a GST-compliant invoice.
How it works
A quotation is prepared with items, quantities, rates from the customer's price list, discounts and taxes,
and has a validity date. If the customer accepts, one click converts it into a sales order without retyping.
The sales order checks stock availability and the customer's credit limit. If either fails, it routes to the
appropriate approver rather than being blocked outright. A confirmed order commits stock — the quantity
is no longer free to sell to someone else, though it is still physically present.
From the sales order, the business either dispatches from stock or raises a work order for production.
Dispatch creates a delivery challan which physically reduces stock. The tax invoice can be raised from
the challan (common for regular supply) or directly from the order (common when goods and invoice travel
together). Partial deliveries and partial invoicing are supported throughout, with each document showing
pending quantities.
