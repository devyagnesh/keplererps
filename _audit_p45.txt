US-M07-03 — As a Store Keeper, I want to receive part of a PO today and the rest next week, so that partial
deliveries are handled properly.
Acceptance: the GRN screen pre-fills pending quantity per line; receiving less leaves the PO partially received;
over-receipt beyond tolerance is blocked.
US-M07-04 — As an Accountant, I want the supplier bill checked against the PO rate and the GRN quantity
before I pay, so that we do not overpay.
Acceptance: a three-way match screen shows PO rate versus bill rate and GRN quantity versus bill quantity,
with differences highlighted; a bill outside tolerance cannot be approved without the 
purchase_bill.approve_mismatch permission and a reason.
US-M07-05 — As a Purchase Manager, I want a supplier rating based on on-time delivery, quality rejection
and price, so that I can drop poor suppliers.
Acceptance: a monthly score per supplier from three weighted components (weights configurable), shown on
the supplier screen with a trend and the underlying data.
