4. Industry Configuration Layer
4.1 The idea in one paragraph
Instead of building eleven products, we build one product with an Industry Profile. During installation the
implementer picks the industry. The profile then switches on the right features, loads the right default
masters, applies the right costing method, adds the right extra fields to the item master, and installs the
right quality parameters and report set. Everything else in the code stays identical.
