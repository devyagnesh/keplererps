Durations assume a team of three developers, one QA engineer and one implementation consultant. Phases 2 and 3 overlap
partially: accounts posting rules are written alongside the sales and purchase modules rather than after them, because
retrofitting accounting into finished documents is far more expensive than building it in.
16.1 Acceptance approach
Stage What happens
Story acceptance Each user story in this document is a test case. QA verifies the acceptance criteria exactly
as written.
Module acceptance The business owner runs their own real transactions through the module and signs off
against the field and validation tables.
Integration acceptanceEnd-to-end scenarios from Section 12 are run with real data, checking that stock,
accounting and alerts all agree.
Parallel run One month of running the ERP alongside the existing method, reconciling stock and sales
figures at the end of the month.
Go-live Opening balances locked, manual records stopped, support arrangement in place for the
first quarter.
