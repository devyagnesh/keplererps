Business rules
# Rule
BR-06 The system must always have at least one active Super Admin. Deactivating or removing the role from the last
one is blocked with a clear message.
BR-07 A user cannot edit their own roles, permissions or data scope, even as Super Admin. This prevents accidental
self-lockout and privilege escalation.
BR-08 System roles (Super Admin) cannot be deleted or renamed; their permission set cannot be reduced.
BR-09 Deleting a user is a soft delete. Their name remains visible on documents they created, shown as "Ramesh
Patel (inactive)".
BR-10 Any change to roles or permissions writes an activity log entry listing exactly which permissions were added
and removed.
MODULE M03
Item Master, UOM, Batch and Serial
Purpose
Defines everything the factory buys, makes, stores and sells. This is the most heavily referenced master in
the system, and the place where the industry profile has the biggest visible effect.
How it works
An item belongs to a type — Raw Material, Consumable, Semi-Finished, Finished Goods, Packing Material,
Scrap, Spare Part or Service. The type controls which sections of the form appear: a Finished Goods item
shows sales price and BOM links; a Raw Material shows purchase settings and reorder levels; a Service item
hides all stock settings. The industry profile adds its own attribute block (cavity count for plastic, GSM for
packaging, alloy for aluminium) as configured fields. Items can carry batch numbers, serial numbers, both,
or neither, and this choice is locked once stock exists.
User stories
US-M03-01 — As a Store Keeper, I want each item to have a unique code and a scannable barcode, so that
receiving and issuing material is fast and error-free.
Acceptance: the item code is auto-generated from a configurable pattern or typed manually; a Code128
barcode is printable from the item screen; scanning the barcode in any item field selects that item.
US-M03-02 — As a Purchase Officer, I want a reorder level and reorder quantity per item per warehouse, so
that the system tells me what to buy before we run out.
Acceptance: when available stock falls to or below the reorder level, alert N06 fires and the item appears on
the "Items to reorder" report with a suggested quantity.
US-M03-03 — As a QC Inspector, I want every chemical batch to carry a manufacturing date and expiry date,
so that expired material is never issued to production.
Acceptance: for items with expiry tracking, issue screens exclude expired batches, warn on batches expiring
within a configurable window, and default to the earliest expiry (FEFO).
US-M03-04 — As a Sales Executive, I want to see the available-to-promise quantity while taking an order, so
that I do not promise stock that is already committed.
Acceptance: the item picker shows physical stock, committed to other orders, in production, and free-to-sell,
per warehouse.
