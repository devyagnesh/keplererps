MODULE M14
HR, Attendance and Payroll
Purpose
Manages the workforce — who is employed, when they worked, what they are owed — with the shift,
overtime and piece-rate patterns that factories actually use.
How it works
Employees are recorded with their department, designation, shift, salary structure and statutory details.
Attendance arrives three ways: from a biometric device file import, from a mobile punch with location, or
by manual entry for staff without devices. The system converts raw punches into attendance days using
shift rules — grace period, half-day threshold, late marks, overtime beyond shift hours, and weekly offs and
holidays from a calendar.
Payroll processing takes attendance plus the salary structure and computes gross pay, statutory deductions
(PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS), advances and loan instalments, and net pay. Piece-rate and production-
linked incentives are supported by reading production entries for the operator. Salary slips are generated
as PDFs and can be delivered on WhatsApp.
User stories
US-M14-01 — As an HR Officer, I want to import the biometric punch file every morning, so that attendance
is captured without typing.
Acceptance: the import accepts the device's CSV or Excel format, matches employees by device code, reports
unmatched punches, and never creates duplicate records for the same employee, date and punch time.
US-M14-02 — As an HR Officer, I want overtime calculated automatically from shift rules, so that wage
disputes are avoided.
Acceptance: hours beyond shift duration, above a configurable minimum threshold, are marked as overtime
and multiplied by the configured rate; the calculation is visible per employee per day.
US-M14-03 — As a Shop Floor Supervisor, I want to pay some operators on a piece-rate basis, so that pay
matches output.
Acceptance: a salary structure component of type "piece rate" reads the good quantity produced by that
operator from production entries for the period, multiplied by the item's piece rate.
US-M14-04 — As an Employee, I want my salary slip on WhatsApp, so that I do not have to ask the office for
a copy.
Acceptance: alert N19 sends a password-protected PDF slip after payroll is approved; the password is the
employee's date of birth in a configured format.
