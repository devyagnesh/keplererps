BR-24 Negative stock is blocked by default. When a business genuinely needs it (goods dispatched before the receipt
is entered), it is enabled per warehouse, and every negative balance appears on a daily exception report.
BR-25 Stock ledger rows are never updated or deleted. A correction is a reversing entry plus a new entry, both linked

BR-26 All stock writes happen inside a database transaction with a row-level lock on the item-warehouse balance, so
two simultaneous issues cannot both consume the last unit.
BR-27 Back-dated entries trigger recalculation of the running balance and valuation for all later entries of that item
and warehouse. This runs as a queued job with the item locked during the rebuild.
BR-28 The total value of the stock ledger must always equal the inventory control account in the general ledger. A
daily reconciliation job checks this and raises an alert on any difference.
MODULE M09
Production Planning and Execution
Purpose
Turns confirmed demand into a plan, the plan into work orders, and work orders into finished goods — while
recording exactly what was consumed, produced, rejected and how long it took.
How it works
The planner starts from demand: confirmed sales orders, forecast quantities, or a make-to-stock decision. A
production plan collects this demand for a period, explodes the BOM to work out what must be made and
what must be bought, compares it with available stock and open purchase orders, and produces two
outputs — a list of work orders to create and a list of shortages to purchase.
Each work order is for one item and one quantity, links to the BOM version in force, lists the operations to
perform, and is assigned to a machine and a target date. Releasing it checks material availability and
reserves stock. On the shop floor the operator records production entries per shift: quantity produced
good, quantity rejected with a defect reason, downtime with a reason, and the operator and machine
involved. Materials are either issued manually before production or backflushed automatically when output
is recorded, depending on the BOM setting.
Closing a work order computes actual cost — material consumed at valuation rate, machine hours at
machine rate, labour hours at labour rate, plus overhead — and compares it against the standard cost from
the BOM, producing a variance the owner can act on.
