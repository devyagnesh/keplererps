US-M13-03 — As an Owner, I want an ageing report showing who owes us money and for how long, so that I
can chase collections.
Acceptance: buckets of 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days, by customer and by invoice, with a total and a
drill-down, exportable and sendable on WhatsApp to the sales team.
US-M13-04 — As an Accountant, I want a GSTR-1 working sheet from the system, so that filing takes an hour
instead of three days.
Acceptance: B2B, B2C large, B2C small, credit and debit notes, exports and HSN summary sections are
produced from confirmed invoices; the totals reconcile to the sales ledger; a JSON or Excel export in the
portal's format is available.
US-M13-05 — As an Owner, I want a profit and loss statement for any date range, so that I can see how the
business is doing without waiting for the year end.
Acceptance: P&L, balance sheet and trial balance for any period, with comparison to the previous period, and
drill-down from any figure to the underlying vouchers.
US-M13-06 — As an Accountant, I want to lock a period once returns are filed, so that nobody changes a
past month by accident.
Acceptance: period locking by date with a per-module override permission; any attempt to post into a locked
period is rejected with a clear message naming the lock date.
