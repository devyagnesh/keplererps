Business rules
# Rule
BR-41 A unit code is globally unique and permanent. It is never reused after the package is opened, consumed,
voided or delivered.
BR-42 The packing unit never holds an authoritative quantity of its own. Its quantity is a dimension recorded on stock
ledger entries; a nightly job reconciles the two and reports any divergence as a defect, not as a stock
adjustment.
BR-43 The sum of quantities packed against a batch may never exceed the quantity produced or received for that
batch.
BR-44 A package cannot be dispatched while its batch is on QC hold or rejected. This block has no override at any
permission level.
