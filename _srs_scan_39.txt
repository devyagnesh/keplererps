MODULE M05
CRM and Lead Management
Purpose
Captures every enquiry from the moment it arrives until it becomes a customer or is lost, so that no
enquiry is forgotten and the owner can see where sales effort is going.
How it works
Leads enter the system from four sources: manual entry, a website enquiry form, a bulk CSV or Excel
upload, or a marketplace API such as IndiaMART or TradeIndia. Each lead is assigned to a sales executive,
either manually or by a round-robin rule. The lead moves through six stages. At every stage the executive
records follow-ups with a next-action date; the system reminds them by push notification on the due date.
A won lead converts into a Party (customer) and optionally straight into a quotation, carrying its details
across without retyping.
