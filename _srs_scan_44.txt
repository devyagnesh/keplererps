Business rules
# Rule
BR-17 An order must contain at least one line. Saving with zero lines is rejected.
BR-18 Confirming an order creates stock commitments per line and warehouse. Cancelling releases them. Delivering
converts commitment into an actual stock reduction.
BR-19 Delivered quantity can never exceed ordered quantity plus a configurable tolerance (default 0%, commonly set
to 2% for continuous processes such as extrusion and textiles).
BR-20 Invoiced quantity can never exceed delivered quantity when invoicing is set to follow delivery.
BR-21 Once any dispatch exists against an order, header fields that affect tax (customer, place of supply, currency)
become read-only.
BR-22 A confirmed invoice is immutable. Corrections are made only through a credit note or debit note, never by
editing the invoice.
BR-23 Invoice numbering must be strictly sequential with no gaps within a financial year, as required by GST rules.
Cancellation keeps the number and marks the invoice cancelled.
MODULE M07
Purchase and Vendor Management
Purpose
Controls what the factory buys and from whom, so that material arrives on time, at an agreed price, from
an approved supplier, and is checked before it is paid for.
How it works
A department raises a purchase indent — a request to buy — which is approved by a manager. Approved
indents can be combined into an RFQ sent to several suppliers. Quotes received are entered and compared
side by side in a comparative statement that highlights the lowest price, best delivery time and best
payment terms. The buyer selects a supplier, and a purchase order is raised, approved according to value
rules, and sent to the supplier by WhatsApp or email as a PDF .
When material arrives, the store keeper creates a goods receipt note against the PO. The GRN records
what  actually  arrived,  which  may  be  less  than  ordered  or  of  a  different  quality.  If  the  item  requires
inspection, the material goes into a quarantine location and stock becomes usable only after QC accepts it.
The supplier's bill is then matched three ways — PO, GRN and bill — and any mismatch in rate or quantity
is flagged before payment.
User stories
US-M07-01 — As a Store Keeper, I want the system to suggest what to buy based on reorder levels and
open orders, so that I do not have to check every item by hand.
Acceptance: a "Purchase suggestion" screen lists items where free stock is at or below the reorder level,
showing current stock, committed quantity, open PO quantity and a suggested order quantity; selected rows
generate an indent in one click.
US-M07-02 — As a Purchase Officer, I want to compare three supplier quotations on one screen, so that I
can justify my choice.
Acceptance: a matrix of items against suppliers showing rate, delivery days, payment terms and total; the
lowest rate per item is highlighted; selecting a supplier per item is allowed (split ordering), and the reason for
not choosing the lowest is mandatory.
