MODULE M11
Machine, Mould and Die Maintenance
Purpose
Keeps the assets that actually make the product running: machines, moulds, dies and tools. In plastic,
rubber, aluminium and steel, the mould or die is often more valuable and more fragile than the machine,
and its life must be tracked in shots or cycles, not only in days.
How it works
Every asset has a register entry with its identification, purchase details, capacity and location. T wo kinds of
maintenance are tracked. Preventive maintenance is scheduled by calendar (every 90 days), by running
hours, or by cycle count (every 50,000 shots for a mould), whichever comes first; the system raises a task
and an alert before it is due.  Breakdown maintenance is logged when something fails: the time it
stopped, the reason, the action taken, spare parts consumed from stock, the time it restarted, and the
resulting downtime cost. Cycle counts increment automatically from production entries, so mould life is
tracked without extra data entry.
User stories
US-M11-01 — As a Maintenance Engineer, I want an alert before a mould reaches its service interval, so
that I service it during a planned stop rather than mid-order.
Acceptance: alert N12 fires at a configurable percentage of the interval (default 90%) and again when due;
the mould appears on the maintenance-due dashboard.
US-M11-02 — As a Production Planner, I want to see which machines are down right now, so that I can
reschedule work orders.
Acceptance: a live machine status board showing Running, Idle, Under breakdown or Under maintenance,
with the affected work orders listed against each stopped machine.
US-M11-03 — As an Owner, I want to know the total downtime and its cost per machine this month, so that I
can decide whether to replace a machine.
Acceptance: a report of downtime hours by reason and machine, valued at the machine's hourly rate plus lost
production value, with a month-on-month trend.
US-M11-04 — As a Maintenance Engineer, I want spare parts issued against a breakdown to come out of
stock, so that spares inventory stays accurate.
Acceptance: the breakdown record has a spare-parts section that creates a stock issue against a maintenance
cost centre.
