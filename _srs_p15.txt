4.5 Costing methods by industry
Method Suits How it works
Weighted Average PVC pipes, plastic, aluminium,
steel, packaging
Every receipt recalculates the average rate: (old
value + new value) ÷ (old qty + new qty). Simple and
stable when raw material prices move gradually.
FIFO Furniture, electrical, auto partsThe oldest received cost layer is consumed first.
Gives an accurate cost when prices move sharply,
and needs a cost-layer table.
Batch / Specific CostChemical, rubber, textile Each batch carries its own cost. Issue picks a specific
batch, so the exact cost of that batch is consumed.
Mandatory when batches differ in potency or shade.
Standard Cost Optional add-on for auto partsA frozen planned cost is used for valuation and the
difference from actual is posted as a variance. Used
when the customer runs formal cost accounting.
Important. The costing method is chosen at installation and locked once the first financial year closes.
Changing it later requires a controlled re-valuation utility with an audit entry, available only to Super Admin.
